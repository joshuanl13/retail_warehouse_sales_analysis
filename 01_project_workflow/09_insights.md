# Insights and Recommendations

## Sales Channel Analysis

### Company Sales Are Highly Dependent on the Warehouse Channel

**Finding**
- Warehouse sales account for 78.27% of the company's total sales
- Retail sales account for 21.73% of the company's total sales.

**Insight**
- Company sales are heavily concentrated within the warehouse sales channel. Although retail sales contributed to a smaller portion of total sales, they still provided an additional source of sales for the company. 

**Recommendation**
- Continue supporting warehouse sales as the primary sales channel while monitoring any opportunities to increase retail sales. A more balanced distribution of sales between channels could diversify total sales performance and reduce reliance on a single sales channel.

## Supplier Analysis

### Sales Are Heavily Concentrated Among the Top Suppliers

**Finding 1**
- Top 10 company suppliers account for 70.43% of the total supplier sales. 
- Top 10 warehouse suppliers account for 78.66% of the total warehouse supplier sales. 
- The top warehouse suppliers consist of the same suppliers as the company's top suppliers in similar ranking order.

**Insight 1**
- With the heavy concentration of sales among top suppliers for the company overall and the warehouse results further support that the warehouse channel is an important source of sales for the company. Additionally, this small group of top suppliers is critical to the company's sales.

**Finding 2**
- Top 10 retail suppliers contain four new suppliers that do not appear within the top warehouse and top company suppliers. 
- The concentration of sales amongst the top 10 retail suppliers accounts for 47.30% of the total retail supplier sales. 

**Insight 2**
- While the concentration of sales amongst the top retail suppliers are still high, it is significantly lower than that of the top warehouse and company suppliers. This lower concentration of sales follows the trend of more diversification in the retail sales channel, although the concentration is still significant. 

**Recommendation**
- For each channel and the company overall, focus on maintaining and strengthening relations with the top suppliers while monitoring potential growth in sales of other suppliers. 

## Item Type Analysis

### Sales Are Heavily Concentrated in Beer and Wine for the Company and Warehouse

**Finding 1**
- For the company overall, beer sales contribute to 70.42% of company sales, while wine contributes 18.88% and liquor contributes 8.90%.
- For the warehouse channel, beer sales contribute to 82.38% of total warehouse sales, while wine contributes 14.60% and kegs contribute 1.49%.

**Insight 1**
- The similarity of heavy concentration of sales in beer and wine for the company and the warehouse further supports the importance of warehouse sales to the company. Additionally the heavy concentration in beer and wine suggests that these two item types are critical to the company.

### Sales Are Distributed More Evenly Amongst the Top Retail Item Types

**Finding 2**
- For retail, liquor contributes to 37.15% of total retail sales, while wine contributes 34.55% and beer contributes 26.57%.
- Liquor appears as top performer in the retail sales as opposed to beer in the warehouse sales. 

**Insight 2**
- Retail item type sales differed significantly from the warehouse and overall company sales. While beer dominated the company and warehouse sales performance, retail sales were more evenly distributed among liquor, beer, and wine with liquor becoming the leading category. This suggests that customer item type preference in the retail channel differs from the warehouse channel and the company overall. 

**Recommendation**
- Continue to focus and maintain beer and wine due to their heavy contribution to overall company sales while highlighting the importance of liquor within the retail channel. Product category decisions should keep in mind performance in each channel due to differing preferences between warehouse and retail sales. 

## Product Analysis

### Sales Are Not Highly Concentrated Among Top Products

**Finding 1** 
- Top 10 company products account for 16.42% of total company sales. 
- Top 10 warehouse products account for 19.57% of total warehouse sales
- The top warehouse products and top company products are the same products in a similar ranking order.

**Insight 1**
- Despite the strong performance of several leading products, sales were not heavily concentrated among that small group of top products. The similarities between the Top 10 overall and Top 10 warehouse products may indicate that warehouse performance may play a large part in the overall company sales performance.

**Finding 2**
- Top 10 retail products account for only 6.87% of total retail sales. 
- Top 10 retail products introduced five products that did not appear within the top warehouse products. 

**Insight 2**
- Within the retail environment, this very small concentration of sales suggests that sales are distributed across an even more diverse selection of products compared to the warehouse channel.

**Recommendation** 
- Keep supporting the Top 10 products in each sales channel while maintaining a large and diverse product assortment.
