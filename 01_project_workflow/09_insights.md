# Insights & Findings

## Sales Channel Analysis

### Company Sales Are Highly Dependent on the Warehouse Channel

**Finding**
- Warehouse sales account for 78.27% of the company's total sales
- Retail sales account for 21.73% of the company's total sales.

**Insight**
- Company sales are heavily concentrated within the warehouse sales channel. Although retail sales contributed to a smaller portion of total sales, they still provided an additional source of sales for the company. 

## Supplier Analysis

### Sales Are Heavily Concentrated Among the Top Suppliers

**Finding 1**
- Top 10 company suppliers account for 70.43% of the total supplier sales. 
- Top 10 warehouse suppliers account for 78.66% of the total warehouse supplier sales. 
- The top warehouse suppliers consist of the same suppliers as the company's top suppliers in similar ranking order.

**Insight 1**
- With the heavy concentration of sales among top company and warehouse suppliers, results further support that the warehouse channel is an important source of sales for the company. Additionally, this small group of top suppliers is highly critical to the company's sales.

**Finding 2**
- Top 10 retail suppliers contain four new suppliers that do not appear within the top warehouse and top company suppliers. 
- The concentration of sales amongst the top 10 retail suppliers accounts for 47.30% of the total retail supplier sales. 

**Insight 2**
- While the concentration of sales amongst the top retail suppliers are still high, it is significantly lower than that of the top warehouse and company suppliers. This lower concentration of sales follows the trend of more diversification in the retail channel, although the concentration is still significant. 

## Item Type Analysis

### Sales Are Heavily Concentrated in Beer and Wine for the Company and Warehouse

**Finding 1**
- For the company overall, beer sales contribute to 70.42% of company sales, while wine contributes 18.88% and liquor contributes 8.90%.
- For the warehouse channel, beer sales contribute to 82.38% of total warehouse sales, while wine contributes 14.60% and kegs contribute 1.49%.

**Insight 1**
- Beer dominates sales for the company and even more in the warehouse channel. While wine contributes a significantly lower number of sales, it is still important as it is the second highest contributor to company and warehouse sales.

### Sales Are Distributed More Evenly Amongst the Top Retail Item Types

**Finding 2**
- For retail, liquor contributes to 37.15% of total retail sales, while wine contributes 34.55% and beer contributes 26.57%.
- Liquor appears as top performer in the retail sales as opposed to beer in the warehouse sales. 

**Insight 2**
- Retail item type sales differed significantly from the warehouse and company sales. While beer dominated the company and warehouse sales performance, retail sales were more evenly distributed among liquor, beer, and wine with liquor becoming the leading category suggesting that customer item type preference in the retail channel differs from the warehouse channel.

## Product Analysis

### Sales Are Not Highly Concentrated Among Top Products

**Finding 1** 
- Top 10 company products account for 16.42% of total company sales. 
- Top 10 warehouse products account for 19.57% of total warehouse sales
- The top warehouse products and top company products are the same products in a similar ranking order.

**Insight 1**
- Despite the strong performance of several leading products, sales were not heavily concentrated among that small group of top products for the company and warehouse.

**Finding 2**
- Top 10 retail products account for only 6.87% of total retail sales. 
- Top 10 retail products introduced five products that did not appear within the top warehouse products. 

**Insight 2**
- Within the retail environment, this very small concentration of sales suggests that sales are distributed across an even more diverse selection of products compared to the warehouse channel.
